About Plato School

Why this school
exists at all

I arrived in the United States at sixteen, in the first year of high school, and was admitted to Harvard three and a half years later. I studied economics, have written twenty-three non-fiction books, and have spoken to roughly a million people across Korea. Two things became impossible to ignore. American higher education has priced itself past the families it claims to serve. And the American students who will lead over the next thirty years are growing up without the East — without the language, the habits, or any working knowledge of the half of the world their careers will run through. Minerva showed that the first problem has a structural answer. Seoul answers the second. Plato is built for both at once.

US non-profit corporation

Incorporated in California. Finances are disclosed annually through IRS Form 990, and any surplus is reinvested in teaching rather than distributed.

Partner university for credit — agreement in progress

Plato is not an accrediting body, is not WSCUC-accredited, and has not applied to WSCUC. The intended structure is a credit-recognition agreement with a US university that already holds that accreditation, so that the transcript is issued in that university name. Until such an agreement is signed, that structure is an intention rather than a fact.

Every AMCAS prerequisite, completed

Admissions committees care less about the name on the transcript than whether the prerequisites were completed. Biology, chemistry, organic chemistry, physics, biochemistry, psychology, sociology and statistics are all built into the programme.

US faculty, taught in hybrid form

Live instruction from US-based faculty is paired with resident instructors in Seoul who handle day-to-day academic support. All coursework is in English.

Eight tracks, and internship placement

Pre-medical is where the school began and the only track whose curriculum is finished. Tracks in AI and computing, game development, the life sciences, pharmaceutical science, the music business, screen production and finance are in preparation. Across every track we work to place students in internships with Korean companies — an objective, not yet a feature.

Tracks

Eight ways through
the same four years

Plato is an undergraduate school, so a track is a course of study and not a separate institution. Every student reads the same core — coursework in English, writing, quantitative reasoning — and then goes deep in one direction. Pre-medical is the track the school was designed around and the only one whose curriculum is finished. The rest are in preparation, and each card says so.

Pre-Medical

Every AMCAS prerequisite, a two-year MCAT sequence, and clinical observation timetabled into the academic week rather than squeezed around it. Ajou University Hospital is adjacent to the campus, and the Gangnam and Seocho medical cluster is about thirty minutes away by rail.

AI & Computing

Machine learning, systems and software engineering, read where the hardware is made. Korea produces a large share of the world’s memory semiconductors, and the engineering employers that depend on them are inside the city rather than a flight away.

In preparation

Game Development

Design, engine programming and production. Korea built one of the largest game industries in the world and was the first country to treat competitive play as a professional sport. The studios and the leagues are both here.

Bio & Life Sciences

Molecular biology, genetics and laboratory method, with research placement as the point of the degree rather than a bonus attached to it. The Songdo biocluster, about an hour from the classroom, holds some of the largest biologics manufacturing capacity anywhere.

In preparation

Pharmaceutical Sciences

Pharmacology, formulation, and the regulatory path a drug actually travels from laboratory to patient. Korea’s contract manufacturing sector and its density of clinical trials give students something to study that is not only a textbook.

In preparation

K-Pop & Music Business

Not performance training. The business behind it: A&R, rights and publishing, touring and live operations, and how a Korean label builds an act for an audience that was never domestic. The industry that exports this music is headquartered in this city.

In preparation

Screen: K-Drama & Film

Writing, production and post for the screen. Korean drama and film now reach a global audience through streaming distribution, and the production companies that make them work out of Seoul. Students read the craft and the rights structure together.

In preparation

Finance

Investment analysis, fund structures, and how capital actually moves, taught where a student can watch it happen. Korea’s institutional investors and venture firms sit inside the same metropolitan area, and unpaid placements with domestic limited and general partners are the point of the track rather than something added to it. Scheduled Bloomberg Terminal time runs through all four years, with the Bloomberg Market Concepts certificate taken in the first year — a credential a recruiter already recognises. Three terminals for a cohort of twenty — a large business school runs twelve for eight thousand. Here the terminal is a desk rather than a facility. Read the full track →

What is settled and what is not. Only the pre-medical curriculum is complete. The other seven tracks have a designed shape but no finished syllabus and no partner agreement specific to them. A student applying today is applying to the pre-medical track. We will open the others when they are ready and not before, and we would rather say that here than discover it with you in the second year. What opens a track is money. The faculty and the syllabus are a hiring problem, and hiring is a budget — which is precisely what we are raising. The list is longer than what we teach today because it is the plan, and we would rather publish the plan than pretend the school is already finished.

Pre-Medical Track — Extracurriculars & Clinical

The experience admissions
committees look for

This section describes the pre-medical track. On a medical school application, clinical exposure and research carry as much weight as scores. Seoul makes both possible inside the undergraduate years rather than after them.

Clinical shadowing

Students observe consultations under the supervision of the attending physician. Korean medical law restricts unlicensed students to observation and learning, so no invasive procedures are performed. We state that limit plainly.

Research and publication

Students are connected to co-authorship on papers and to conference presentations. The aim is substance in the AMCAS Work and Activities section, not a list of titles.

Service and community health

Programmes serve communities with limited access to care. Committees read for sustained commitment and initiative rather than for the size of any single activity.

Dedicated MCAT preparation

Diagnostic testing, an individual study plan and regular full-length practice exams run across two years. The MCAT is scored against a single national standard regardless of where a student studied.

AMCAS and MMI preparation

Application strategy, the personal statement, and full rehearsal of the Multiple Mini Interview format now used by roughly 60% of North American medical schools.

A considered application list

There are 155 MD schools in the United States. We tier the ones realistically open to international applicants and build each student a list from that, rather than applying broadly and hoping.
